Data Sources

  1. NFL Weekly Injury Reports (2010–2026): 88,730 weekly injury records from nflverse/nflverse-data, covering player names, teams, positions, primary injuries, and game statuses across 17 seasons.
  2. NFL Playing Surface Analytics (elijah24/nfl-injuries): 105 detailed lower-extremity injury records (InjuryRecord.csv) and 267,005 play records (PlayList.csv) comparing Natural vs. Synthetic turf surfaces and injury severity (DM_M1 through DM_M42).
  3. Multimodal Sports Injury Telemetry (anjalibhegam/multimodal-sports-injury-dataset): Biometric and biomechanical sensor readings including heart rate, ground reaction force (GRF), joint flexion angles, fatigue index, recovery score, and hydration level.
  4. U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey (tidycensus): Live state-level population, median household income, and median age fetched via the Census API for each NFL team’s home state.
