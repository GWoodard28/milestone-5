Project Overview

This project combines 88,835 NFL injury records from 2010 through 2026 (nflverse-data and the NFL Playing Surface Analytics study) with multimodal athlete sensor telemetry and live annual U.S. Census Bureau (ACS) state demographics via tidycensus.
